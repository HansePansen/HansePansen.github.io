The essentials

The app does not need an account with us. Your scans and settings stay on your devices and in your own iCloud. CREABLO GmbH receives none of it. The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and passes no data to third parties except to the services you choose to use (described below). In the Android app, Google’s text recognition additionally sends technical diagnostic data to Google (see “Android app”).

Scans and settings

The app saves scans as PDF files on your device and – if iCloud Drive is turned on – in your iCloud Drive, so they are the same on all your devices with the same Apple ID. Settings (display name, templates, unlocks, the app’s ID) are also kept on the device and in your iCloud. iCloud is provided by Apple; Apple’s privacy policy applies. We have no access to your iCloud.

Camera, photos and files

The app uses the camera only to scan documents and QR codes. It reads photos and files only when you select them. Cropping, text recognition and detecting the document type happen on the device.

Location

Only if you turn on “Include location” does the app ask for your approximate location, to write the place (city) into the name of a scan. Apple’s map service determines the place name from the location. The location is not stored; only the place name appears in the file name.

Signing in with Microsoft and Google, sending email

Employees of CREABLO GmbH can sign in with their Microsoft account. If you wish, you can connect your own Microsoft or Gmail account to send scans directly through templates. Sign-in is handled by Microsoft or Google; the app receives your name, your email address and an access token, which is kept only in your device’s keychain (Android: the app’s protected storage). The app sends emails through your mailbox (Microsoft Graph or Gmail API) to the recipients of your template; afterwards they are in your sent items. The privacy policies of Microsoft and Google apply.

Through “Apple Mail” or the share menu the app opens other apps on your device; what happens there is governed by their providers.

Google user data (Gmail)

If you connect a Gmail account, the app requests these permissions from Google: your email address and basic profile (to show which account is connected) and “Send email on your behalf” (Gmail API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send).

  • Use. The app uses this access only to send the emails you trigger yourself – a scan you share through one of your templates, to the recipients you entered. It does not read, list, change or delete any of your emails, contacts or other Google data.
  • Storage. The access token and your email address are stored only on your device. The email is composed on your device and sent directly to Google. Nothing passes through or is stored on servers of CREABLO GmbH.
  • Sharing. We do not transfer, sell or share Google user data. It is not used for advertising, not read by humans and not used to develop, improve or train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • Revoking and deleting. In the app, “Disconnect Gmail” (under “Manage Templates”) deletes the token from your device; on Android it also revokes the app’s access at Google. You can revoke the access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. On Android, uninstalling the app also deletes the token; on iPhone, iPad and Mac, disconnect Gmail in the app before deleting it.

The app’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Checking for new versions and unlocks

When opened, the app checks whether a newer version exists – in TestFlight via a small file on GitHub Pages (provider GitHub, Inc.), in the App Store via Apple’s directory. As with any request on the internet, your IP address is transmitted to the respective server; the app sends no other data.

Purchases

In-app purchases (e.g. Premium) are processed by Apple. We receive no payment data.

Android app

For the app from Google Play, everything above applies with these differences:

  • Storage location. Scans are kept only on your device, in the folder “Documents/Superscanner” (on Android 10 in the app’s storage) – you can also see them in the Files app. Settings are kept in the app’s storage. There is no iCloud sync on Android.
  • Text recognition and QR codes. The Android app uses Google ML Kit for this. Images and recognised text stay on the device. ML Kit sends technical usage and diagnostic data to Google, such as device model, Android and app version, run times, error codes and a random installation ID. Google uses it to operate and improve the service. Google’s privacy policy applies.
  • Location. Your Android device’s geocoding service (usually from Google) determines the place name from the approximate location. The location is not stored; only the place name appears in the file name.
  • Sign-in and sending. The access tokens from Microsoft and Google are kept only in the app’s protected storage on your device. Instead of “Apple Mail”, the app opens a mail app on your device.
  • New versions. Updates come through Google Play; the app does not fetch a file of its own for this.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases (Premium) are processed by Google Play. We receive no payment data.
  • Deleting. You delete settings and sign-ins by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android’s settings. Your scans stay in the folder “Documents/Superscanner” so they are not lost; you delete them there with the Files app. If you reinstall the app, it asks once whether it may open the folder again.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Since we do not store any personal data about you through the app, you can delete the app’s data yourself by deleting the app and its files in iCloud Drive. For questions, write to info@creablo.de. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.
